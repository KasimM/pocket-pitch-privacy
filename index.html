Pocket Pitch privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version. Your club and your progress are saved on your phone. Your birth year stays on your phone too. The game shows ads from Google. If you are 13 or over, you can choose whether Google may personalise those ads and whether the game may send anonymous game stats and crash reports. If you are under 13, ads are never personalised and no stats or crash reports are sent.

Who we are

Pocket Pitch is made by Kasim Majeed Lubbad, who is responsible for your data under this policy. Contact: pocketpitchgame@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your game save: your club name, players, coins and progress. It is stored on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • Your birth year: asked once, at first launch, only to decide which privacy rules apply. It never leaves your phone.
  • Your settings: sound, notifications and privacy choices.

Notifications from the game are made on your phone. No server sends them, and they are only sent if you turn them on.

Players under 13

If the birth year you enter means you could be under 13, the game treats you as under 13. Then ads are not personalised, ad requests are marked as child-directed, game stats and crash reports are off, and the consent form is not shown.

Your choices

  • Ads and stats choices: open the club crest, then Settings, then Privacy. Where the consent form applies, "Privacy choices" lets you change your answer.
  • Game stats: the "Share game stats" switch in Settings, Privacy turns stats and crash reports on or off.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in Settings, or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your save and your birth year from your phone. To ask us to delete game stats and crash reports linked to your copy of the game, use "Delete my data" in Settings, Privacy, or email pocketpitchgame@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

How long data is kept

Game stats are kept for 2 months, and crash reports for 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically. Ad data is kept by Google under its own policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. Email us to use these rights. If you are in the UK or the EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
